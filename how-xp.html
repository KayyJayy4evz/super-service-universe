📖 How XP Works

Every month (or week) your stats come in from the team dashboard and turn into XP. Here's exactly how.

⚡ Everyday work

SkillWhat countsXP
Call/Chat HandledEach inbound call, outbound call, or customer chat1 each
Emails RespondedCustomer case emails sent½ each (1 per 2)
Problem TouchesEach problem resolved1 each
PaceContacts handled per day worked10 per contact/day

Why pace? It rewards how much you get done on the days you work, so PTO or a short month doesn't sink you. Example: 25 contacts a day = 250 XP.

You earn the highest quality tier you reach, plus Clean Sheet if you qualify. Vesuvius tiers are 0.25 points higher (98.25%, 99.25%, 99.75%).

💬 Customer feedback (tNPS)

BonusEarn it withXP
tNPS Beat ChannelYour tNPS score is higher than your channel's average for the month, with at least 3 surveys+150

Why compare to your channel? Some channels get 8x more surveys than others, and customers rate channels differently. Comparing you to your own channel means a phone rep and a messaging rep have the same shot. Your channel is the skill where you handled the most contacts.

🏆 How teams are ranked

The Overall board ranks teams by average XP per rep, not total. Bigger teams don't win just by having more people.

  • 👔 Managers appear on their team but aren't counted in the average.
  • 💤 No data means no stats came in for you that period. You're not counted in the average, so it never drags your team down.

❓ Quick answers

When does my XP update?

When your manager uploads the month's (or week's) stats. Your XP for that period appears all at once.

My numbers changed after an upload. Why?

If stats are re-uploaded for the same period, the new numbers replace the old ones. XP is never counted twice.

Does a detractor survey take XP away?

No. Nothing in Super Service World takes XP away. A low score just means you don't earn that month's tNPS bonus.

I was out part of the month. Am I behind?

Less than you'd think. Pace and quality bonuses are based on the days you worked, not how many.
